## Interpretation and limitations

1. The player identity is strong: both scans matched the same UID.
2. The `+0x258` value increased while the visible match-sharpness label moved from "Lacking Sharpness" to "Gaining Sharpness". This is a correlation from one before/after observation, not proof that this field is match sharpness.
3. The `+0x25A` signed value changed from -224 to -146. It remains an unlabelled candidate.
4. The `+0x25C` value fell from 10,000 to 8,400. The follow-up included an in-match bruised-heel injury and the visible energy label remained "Fresh". This field must not be labelled condition or fatigue without further controlled tests.
5. A single post-match observation cannot separate effects of match minutes, training, match events, injury, time progression or game recalculation.
6. Previously suggested candidate offsets from external/community research are not automatically valid for this exact Windows FM27 build.

## Data policy for R3.6

- Keep player identity, club linkage and existing attribute extraction separate from fitness interpretation.
- Store raw candidate values and provenance.
- Do not display an unverified candidate as Condition, Match Sharpness, Fatigue or Jadedness.
- Do not use the candidate values for recruitment recommendations or player availability calculations.
- Keep full medical record extraction out of scope.
- Keep all game-memory access read-only (`PROCESS_QUERY_INFORMATION | PROCESS_VM_READ`).
- Do not enable bulk live imports until the module fingerprint and each imported field have an explicit validation status.

## Next validation protocol

Use at least two additional players with no active injury warning, ideally:
- a rested player whose in-game sharpness is low;
- a regular player whose in-game sharpness is high.

Capture screenshot and linked-memory snapshot close together, then repeat after a natural game-state change. For fatigue/jadedness, wait for a naturally tired player rather than deliberately overplaying an injured player. Record player UID, club UID, in-game labels, date/time, match minutes, injury status, module hash and raw offsets.

A field can be promoted to verified only after repeated observations across distinct players/states, a consistent scale, and no contradictory evidence.
